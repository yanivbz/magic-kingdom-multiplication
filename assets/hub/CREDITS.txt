Illustrated home-hub art for עומר והממלכה הקסומה.
- island.webp, girl.webp, avatar.webp, cloud.webp: original illustrations
  created for this app. The character is an illustration only (not a photograph).
- english-house.webp: illustrated ABC cottage, white studio background removed,
  used as the English station sprite.
Do not add photographs of real people to this folder or the public site.
- girl-walk.webp: 4-frame walk cycle derived from an illustrated walk sheet
  (white background removed). Opposite-step frames were built by swapping
  the lower legs below the dress hem so the stride reads clearly. Illustration
  only, not a photograph.
